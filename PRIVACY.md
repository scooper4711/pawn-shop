# Pawn Shop Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. This policy covers Pawn Shop for Mac, iPad and iPhone.

**Pawn Shop collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party
code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else. The only network
requests the App Store version makes to the internet are to your own iCloud, described below.

## What stays on your device

- **The PDFs you import** are read on your device to pull out their art. They are kept in the app's storage there
  and are never uploaded.
- **The sheets you make** are files you save where you choose.
- **Your settings** are saved on your device.

## Your library in iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Pawn Shop, the app keeps your pawn library the same on your
devices through your private iCloud database: the art it pulled out of your PDFs, the names and keywords of
pawns, and the custom pawns you made, with their images. The PDFs themselves are not uploaded. Turnkeeper and
Field Guide, the developer's other apps, share this library if you use them.

Only you can read your private iCloud database. The developer cannot see it, and Apple's privacy policy covers
how Apple stores it. To stop syncing, turn iCloud off for Pawn Shop in your device's settings. To delete what was
uploaded, use Manage Storage in your device's iCloud settings; Pawn Shop then stops syncing on that device until
you turn it back on in Settings › Sync.

Deleting the app from your Mac leaves its data behind: macOS keeps an app's saved data in the Containers folder of
your Library folder until you delete the folder named Pawn Shop there.

## Tagging with Ollama

Pawn Shop can suggest keywords and names for pawns by asking Ollama, a program you run on your own Mac. Tagging is
off until you turn it on in Settings › Tagging. The pictures go only to the server named there, your own Mac
unless you change it yourself, and that server is yours; the developer sees nothing.

## Other apps on your Mac

Pawn Shop can import the PDFs Scrollkeeper, a separate app, has downloaded, through a folder the apps share.
Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does.

## The disk image version

The version downloaded from GitHub can check GitHub for a newer release when it opens, if you leave that setting
on. The check sends GitHub the same request any web browser would; the developer sees nothing of it.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Pawn Shop to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Pawn Shop collects no data from anyone, including children.
