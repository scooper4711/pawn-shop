# Pawn Shop Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers Pawn Shop for Mac.

**Pawn Shop collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party
code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else. The App Store
version makes no network requests to the internet.

## What stays on your Mac

- **The PDFs you import** are read on your Mac to pull out their art. The art, your pawn library and the sheets you
  make are kept in the app's storage on your Mac and in files you save. They are never uploaded.
- **Your settings** are saved on your Mac.

Deleting the app from your Mac leaves this behind: macOS keeps an app's saved data in the Containers folder of
your Library folder until you delete the folder named Pawn Shop there.

## Tagging with Ollama

Pawn Shop can suggest keywords and names for pawns by asking Ollama, a program you run on your own Mac. The pictures
go only to the address in your settings, `localhost` unless you change it yourself, and that server is yours;
the developer sees nothing. Turn tagging off in Settings › Tagging.

## Other apps on your Mac

Pawn Shop can import the PDFs Scrollkeeper, a separate app, has downloaded, through a folder the apps share.
Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does.

## The disk image version

The version downloaded from GitHub can check GitHub for a newer release when it opens, if you leave that setting
on. The check sends GitHub the same request any web browser would; the developer sees nothing of it.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Pawn Shop to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Pawn Shop collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here, with a new effective date. Questions go to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/scooper4711/pawn-shop/issues).
